## Maruti Suzuki Sales Analytics Dashboard

## Overview:
This project is a Sales Analytics Dashboard built using Excel, SQL, and Power BI to analyze Maruti Suzuki sales performance from 2020 to 2025.
The project transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights by analyzing revenue, profit, dealer performance, customer satisfaction, sales trends, and product performance.

## Tools Used : 
- Microsoft Excel
- SQL
- Power BI

## Dataset Information:
Records: 793,792 Rows
Columns: 20
Period: 2020 - 2025

- Sales Date
- Product Information
- Dealer Information
- State
- Fuel Type
- Transmission
- Units Sold
- Revenue
- Profit Approximation
- Customer Satisfaction
- Finance Provider
- Payment Mode

- Total Profit Approximation: ₹1,187.79 Cr

## Business Recommendations:
i) Focus on High Revenue States:
Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Gujarat contribute significantly to revenue. Marketing and inventory planning should prioritize these regions.

ii) Increase Availability of Top Models:
Fronx, S-Presso, Jimmy, Grand Vitara, and Brezza are among the top-selling models. Ensuring stock availability can maximize sales opportunities.

iii) Improve Low Performing Dealers:
Dealer performance analysis can help identify dealerships requiring operational improvements and additional support.

iv) Strengthen Customer Satisfaction:
Maintaining an average satisfaction score above 84 should remain a key business objective through better service and customer engagement.

v) Expand High Demand Segments:
Hatchback and SUV segments contribute the majority of sales. Future product strategies should focus on these categories.
